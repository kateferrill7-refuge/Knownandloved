First Steps to Freedom is a free video series for Christian women who are beginning to find their way out of the confusion and pain of narcissistic abuse. Short, gentle, and made to be watched at your own pace.

The video series

Teaching that helps you name what happened, understand why it was so hard to see, and take the first steady steps toward freedom.

Weekly emails

A calm, encouraging note each week with the next step, so you are never left carrying this alone.

Handouts

Simple, printable pages to go with each lesson. Write on them, pray over them, and come back to them.

This series is for you if

  • You keep asking yourself, "Is it me?"
  • You are walking on eggshells in a relationship
  • You have left, or are thinking about leaving
  • You feel confused about what forgiveness requires
  • You want healing that honors your faith
  • You are tired of being told to try harder

Hi, I'm Kate.

I am a Certified Abuse Recovery Coach with six years of experience walking alongside Christian women as they recover from painful relationships. I am also pursuing my graduate degree in Marriage and Family Therapy.

I built this series because I know how lonely the first steps can feel. My work is warm, faith-centered, and trauma-informed, and it starts from one truth: you are known, you are loved, and you are not too broken to heal.

A note on safety

If you are in immediate danger, please call 911. The National Domestic Violence Hotline is available 24/7 at 1-800-799-7233, or by text: start to 88788. Coaching and educational videos are not a substitute for emergency or clinical care.
