What Is Reclaim Your Self

Healing That Reaches Your Whole Person

There is a version of healing that lives only in your head. You understand the psychology, you've traced the patterns, you know exactly what happened and why. And then a text arrives, or a certain smell catches you off guard — and your body responds as if none of that understanding ever happened. That is not failure. That is trauma stored where insight cannot reach it: in your nervous system, in your body.

"Be transformed by the renewing of your mind."

Romans 12:2 — the Greek word for "mind" includes the whole inner life: will, emotion, perception, and felt experience. Transformation is whole-person.

Reclaim Your Self is a 12-week faith-based, trauma-informed course that takes you through four phases of healing — Foundation, Processing, Reclamation, and Integration — using somatic practices, Scripture, and interactive tools designed to reach where healing actually needs to happen. Not just your mind. All of you.
